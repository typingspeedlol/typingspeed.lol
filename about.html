About typingspeed.lol

typingspeed.lol is a free, browser-based typing speed test. It measures your net WPM and accuracy in four modes, and runs entirely in your browser.

The site was built by Pratik Nilange, a hobbyist developer, as a fun way to practise typing. You can read more about his work at pratiknilange.in.

Game modes

  • Sprint (15s / 30s / 60s): a timed test of net WPM and accuracy, with live personal-best pacing.
  • Sudden Death: one typo ends the run. The pace timer starts after word 3 and tightens gradually.
  • Chaos .lol: 25 words from developer, terminal and tech-culture vocabulary.
  • Zen Mode: a low-glare dark theme with the interface dimmed until you hover over it.

How your data is handled

WPM is calculated with the standard five-characters-per-word convention. Keystrokes are processed in your browser and are never uploaded. Only your personal bests and preferences are saved, on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
