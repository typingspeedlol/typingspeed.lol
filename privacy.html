Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

typingspeed.lol is a free typing test run by Pratik Nilange. This policy explains what is stored and shared when you use the site. No account is required, and your keystrokes are never sent to a server.

1. Data stored on your device

We use your browser's local storage to save your personal best scores, your sound preference and your cookie consent choice. You can clear this at any time in your browser's site data settings.

2. Cookies and Google AdSense

We show advertisements from Google AdSense on the results screen only. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings or at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's partner-sites policy.
  • Where required by law, including for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. Choose "Non-Personalized" on the banner to opt out.

3. Analytics

We do not currently use third-party analytics tools on typingspeed.lol.

4. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data. We hold no account data, so clearing your browser storage removes most of it. For any other request, email hello@typingspeed.lol.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
